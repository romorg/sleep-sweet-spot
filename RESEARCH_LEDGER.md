# Research ledger: tested, provisional, deferred

This ledger is kept to avoid selectively displaying only attractive results. It does not introduce the later prospective diary into the retrospective dataset.

| Avenue | Current evidence | Decision for Stage 1 release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timing, end time, duration | Verified against historical source and reproducible pipeline | **Main finding** |
| Following-night transitions | Verified calendar adjacency, pair counts and three-window medians | **Main finding**, no recovery claim |
| Split episodes / architecture | Explicit 60/90/120-minute sensitivity definitions and stage-quality filters | **Main finding** using 1,381 unexcluded stage windows |
| Clock vs Sleep++ vs Watch | Multiple applications and recording formats | **Data-quality finding**, not a pooled trend |
| Pulse and HRV | A substantive earlier internal study with 1,239 nights and uncertainty intervals exists, but its separate cardiac inputs and scripts were not re-executed in this closure | **Deferred**: no public empirical effect estimate |
| Lunar phase | A later independent exploratory reanalysis was sensitive to bin definitions and comparison calendars | **Deferred**: no claim of a stable lunar effect or proof of zero effect |
| Magnesium | Nonrandomized observational comparison with uncertain exact exposure timing | **Deferred**: no supplement efficacy claim |
| Daytime energy, clarity and fatigue | Standardized diary commenced only after the October 1 historical cutoff | **Stage 2 only**, not analyzed or published here |

For Stage 1, a project is *complete* when the defensible retrospective question, sample rules, estimates, alternatives and limitations are explicit. It does not require claiming answers to every exploratory question raised along the way.
